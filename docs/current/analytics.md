# Analytics

This specification owns accepted analytical semantics. Its metric set remains provisional and changes only through an explicitly approved increment. [The index](index.md) defines the documentation route.

## Current contract

### Canonical evidence and calculation ownership

- Maneuver analysis operates on an Activity's complete canonical normalized track, with GPS/UTC time and available SOG, COG, HDG, HEEL and TRIM. Missing or non-finite telemetry is unavailable; it is not reconstructed from weather context, another sensor or visual samples. HDG is distinct from COG and is never inferred from it. Circular headings and differences respect the 0°/360° boundary. HEEL/TRIM signs have no inferred port/starboard or bow-up/bow-down meaning.
- The backend owns maneuver detection, maneuver metrics and persisted maneuver artifacts. The frontend consumes the authorized analytical API and does not calculate maneuver events or read artifact files. Existing Viewer Summary, VS distributions and chart presentation are frontend calculations over the received canonical samples; their user-facing behavior belongs in [Session Viewer](session-viewer.md).
- Any visual reduction, smoothing or aggregation for map and chart rendering is presentation-only. It does not reduce the valid canonical sample population used for analytical calculations. [Ingestion and Data](ingestion-and-data.md) owns canonical Activity data and source normalization; [Session Context](session-context.md) owns modelled weather and marine context, which is not an analytical input.

### Neutral maneuver detection and event boundaries

- The delivered HIGH detector requires canonical HDG. It splits continuous evidence at gaps longer than 1.5 seconds, uses circular-safe local heading smoothing and turn rate, and requires at least 20 valid HDG samples in an eligible continuous segment. It seeds a candidate at an absolute turn rate of at least 4°/s and follows directional rotation at 0.5°/s. An accepted event lasts 3–30 seconds and must satisfy the current heading-change, directional-coherence, sample-integrity and available supporting COG/HEEL/SOG checks. These values describe the accepted provisional detector, not a general sailing standard or a promise that every physical maneuver is found.
- The existing HDG-primary detector is **HIGH**. Normal selection prefers any available HIGH result, including zero events and sufficient HDG without HEEL. Only unavailable HIGH evidence permits **LOW**, the accepted separate Phases detector restricted to canonical UTC, COG and SOG. HEEL alone cannot select HIGH; HDG cannot replace COG for LOW. TRIM remains diagnostic in HIGH; quaternion is not used in detection. Neither detector derives telemetry from positions or modifies canonical data.
- LOW evaluates every valid continuous run. Missing/non-finite COG or SOG, negative SOG, invalid/non-increasing UTC and gaps **over 10 seconds** split evidence. Finite COG is circularly normalized. The unchanged Phases baseline circularly smooths COG over ±2 seconds and calculates local circular rate from real samples nearest the ±2-second targets (earlier UTC wins ties), divided by actual elapsed time. Seeds require absolute rate ≥2°/s and SOG ≥1.5 kn. Consecutive seeds at most 3 seconds apart delimit a 3–35 second interval. At least three valid smoothed courses are required in each context window: **[start−10s, start−2s)** and **(end+2s, end+10s]**. Circular post/pre mean change must be at least 35°, directional coherence at least 0.65, and median interval SOG at least 1.5 kn. No SOG drop or stable entry/exit phase is required.
- LOW availability is independent of turn acceptance: at least one run must support potential seed endpoints 3–35 seconds apart at SOG ≥1.5 kn with calculable local rates, a valid smoothed interval, and the required nondegenerate circular context. Rate, angle, coherence and interval median-speed rejection can then yield an **available zero-event** result, including straight sailing. No coverage percentage or global GPS noise threshold is introduced. Stationary missing bearings remain missing; zero/low SOG cannot seed turns. Finite noisy bearings cannot be declared reliable from GPS accuracy absent from the canonical inputs.
- When neither detector can execute, analytics is **unavailable** with no mode or events. LOW assessment reports absent required columns as `missing_utc`, `missing_cog` or `missing_sog`; otherwise it checks, in order: no valid UTC (`invalid_time`), wholly unusable COG then SOG (`missing_cog`/`missing_sog`), no jointly valid continuous run (`insufficient_low_evidence`), no sample at seed-eligible speed (`insufficient_low_speed`), or insufficient interval/context (`insufficient_low_context`). Missing/non-finite values break runs rather than being dropped or filled. Later valid runs remain independently eligible.
- Each accepted event has start and end GPS/UTC timestamps delimiting its directional core. Its HIGH center is the cumulative-rotation midpoint and is the sole event navigation time; it need not equal the temporal midpoint. Events annotate the existing Activity. They do not create Activities, Sessions, races, legs or Saved Segments.
- The event remains a **neutral maneuver candidate** or significant sailing-direction change. TackBar does not label it a tack or gybe without separately validated and approved classification. The detector provides evidence for a debrief, not proof of tactical meaning, success or failure.
- HIGH retains its cumulative signed-rotation center, strictly inside the event. LOW uses the first canonical sample reaching half the accumulated absolute circular rotation within its seed interval; at coarse cadence this sample may equal the event end (`start < center <= end`). Both use the center as the sole navigation time.

### Delivered maneuver measures

- A maneuver result reports start, center and end time, duration, mode-specific circular direction change, entry/minimum/exit SOG, recovery time, Loss distance and equivalent Loss time when their inputs support them. Missing metric inputs yield `null` in the API and an unavailable value in the Viewer. This set remains evidence-driven and provisional; a new or revised measure requires an explicit approved contract and focused validation.
- The angle is mode-specific: HIGH retains `heading_change_deg` as HDG change, with `course_change_deg: null`; LOW exposes circular pre/post COG change as `course_change_deg`, with `heading_change_deg: null`. HIGH retains its calculated absolute local HDG peak turn rate in °/s and its UTC timestamp. LOW's `peak_turn_rate_deg_s` and `peak_turn_rate_time` are both null: its local seed rate is calculated, but the delivered event does not calculate a peak. Angle and peak fields remain explicit through artifacts and API, with unavailable values null. Existing SOG metrics apply independently to each mode using actual event start/end and their own validity rules.
- **Entry SOG** is the median of valid, finite, non-negative canonical SOG from 25 to 8 seconds before event start, inclusive. **Minimum SOG** is the minimum valid SOG within the event. **Exit SOG** is the median from 25 to 45 seconds after event end. Recovery requires comparable entry and exit regimes: their difference cannot exceed the greater of 0.75 kt and 15% of entry SOG. **Recovery time** ends at the first valid sample from event end through 60 seconds afterward with SOG at least 95% of exit SOG.
- **Loss distance** integrates the positive deficit below the *constant entry SOG* from event start through the recovery sample, using piecewise-linear canonical SOG and actual sample intervals, with 1 kt = 0.514444 m/s. Speed above entry contributes zero, not a negative loss or gain. **Equivalent Loss time** is that distance divided by entry SOG in m/s. Loss time requires positive entry SOG, a comparable exit regime, recovery and at least two valid SOG samples in the Loss interval; otherwise it remains unavailable. Loss is a debrief speed-deficit measure, not tactical gained/lost, VMG, opponent-relative performance or a maneuver score.

### Individual maneuver profile foundation

- The frontend can extract a raw, individual SOG, COG and HEEL profile from an existing event and its Activity's canonical samples. An event is eligible when its center is inside the shared Analysis Window, including either boundary. Its nominal relative-time span is `[-25s, +25s]` around that center; the permitted interval is the intersection with the Analysis Window. Only original valid samples within the permitted interval become profile points, with their original UTC timestamp, relative offset and value. SOG stays in knots, COG retains its original circular degree value, and HEEL retains signed degrees. No resampling, interpolation, smoothing, aggregate rotation, artificial endpoint or persisted curve is produced.
- Each signal independently retains available points and marks whether a point may connect to the preceding valid point. Connection requires adjacent canonical samples with valid signal values and a positive UTC interval no longer than 1.5 seconds. Missing/non-finite values, invalid or non-increasing UTC and larger gaps break continuity without deleting valid endpoints. Represented time sums eligible adjacent valid UTC intervals clipped to the permitted interval. Per-signal coverage reports that time against both the permitted interval duration and the nominal 50 seconds; a zero-duration permitted interval yields empty points and zero coverage. Partial profiles have no 80% eligibility cutoff. This foundation does not add a chart or alter detection, event boundaries or Viewer state. The separate typical SOG and COG aggregation rules follow below.

### Typical SOG aggregation foundation

- For one Activity, detected events are considered only when their centers lie in the Analysis Window. An event's individual SOG profile must have at least 65% represented-time coverage against the full nominal 50 seconds to contribute to a typical curve. This is independent of the VS 80% threshold. Clipped and partial individual profiles remain available even when excluded from aggregation.
- Each coverage-eligible event contributes at most one SOG value in knots at each of 51 inclusive one-second offsets from `-25s` to `+25s`. An exact valid original sample is used when present. Otherwise, linear interpolation is permitted only between adjacent valid SOG profile points joined by #11.1 continuity, whose source UTC interval is positive and at most 1.5 seconds. Interpolation cannot use a sample outside the Analysis Window, bridge a gap or create an endpoint. These grid values are derived only for aggregation; they do not change the individual raw profile or canonical track.
- The unsmoothed typical value at each offset is the median of its contributing maneuver values, with each maneuver weighted once regardless of sample cadence. Null and contributor count are retained at unsupported offsets. A typical median curve is available only with at least three coverage-eligible maneuvers; zero yields no curve, while one or two yields an explicit insufficient-maneuvers state and no representative median. The result distinguishes detected, window-eligible and coverage-eligible counts. The utility is called independently for P and C; it does not pair events. The visible filter and per-offset drawing threshold are defined below.

### Visible SOG and COG profile comparison

- The Viewer derives a common filter-only `|ΔCOG|` for each HIGH or LOW event from canonical COG and valid SOG in the current Analysis Window. It uses the LOW Phases contextual method: runs break on invalid UTC/COG/SOG, negative SOG, non-increasing UTC or gaps over 10 seconds; each COG sample is circularly smoothed over ±2 seconds within its run. At least three valid smoothed courses are needed in each `[start−10s, start−2s)` and `(end+2s, end+10s]` context. The circular pre/post means yield the absolute change. Context and smoothing use no source sample outside the Analysis Window; clipped context may therefore make the filter value unavailable. No HDG or persisted LOW angle is substituted. This derived value does not replace reported event angles, detector mode or artifacts.
- The common inclusive angular band starts at 40°–100°, has 5° steps and provisional 0°–180° bounds. Events without a common contextual change are excluded with a visible count. The selected band filters prepared per-event SOG or COG grids before independent P/C aggregation; band interaction does not re-extract canonical tracks. Each displayed median offset requires at least three contributing coverage-eligible maneuvers. Raw is the unsmoothed median; graphical smoothing follows aggregation and breaks at unsupported offsets. The chart has a shared scale for the selected signal, a fixed ±25-second axis and `t=0` marker. HEEL aggregation, per-offset weak-support interpretation beyond this drawing threshold and validated mobile visual density remain future work.
- The COG option shows relative course turn in degrees, never median geographic COG. It uses the same valid, Analysis Window-clipped, circularly smoothed pre/post context as the common filter to establish entry and exit course references. The shortest signed entry-to-exit change sets orientation; a zero or exactly 180° change has no unambiguous direction and yields no COG curve. Each individual #11.1 COG profile keeps its original samples and continuity. For the aggregate only, connected points unwrap by shortest adjacent circular changes; a disconnected section restarts from the shortest displacement relative to entry, without joining the gap. An exactly 180° point step is omitted as ambiguous. Oriented values subtract half the entry-to-exit magnitude, making the references negative and positive respectively. Event `center_time` remains `t=0` even if the angular zero crossing occurs elsewhere. The COG grid follows the existing exact-or-valid-bracketed 1-second interpolation rule and independent ≥65% nominal COG coverage rule; the median is per offset across eligible maneuvers. COG smoothing follows this median without joining unsupported sections. The COG chart uses a symmetric degree axis around zero. Profile recovery remains SOG-only.
- COG Insights use only supported offsets of each Activity's unsmoothed relative COG median. Entry and exit levels are medians of supported values in −25s…−15s and +15s…+25s respectively, each requiring at least 8 of 11 offsets. Turn angle is exit minus entry when positive. The central turn search spans −15s…+15s. Turn midpoint is the first confirmed 0° upward crossing connected to a supported 10–90% progression; for unequal entry and exit levels, it may precede 10% or follow 90%. Turn start and end are crossings of 10% and 90% of the entry-to-exit angle. Crossings interpolate only between adjacent supported one-second offsets and require two consecutive supported seconds at or beyond the crossed level. A return below 10% invalidates an earlier start, a return below 0° invalidates an earlier midpoint, and an unsupported gap between start and end makes midpoint and duration unavailable. Angle can remain available when duration is not. Three compact P/C Insights cards show Turn angle, Turn midpoint and Turn duration (10–90%), with neutral displayed differences when both roles have evidence. The chart has no COG duration annotation. These values do not change event-table angles or Recovery.
- Maneuver Insights use only supported offsets of each Activity's unsmoothed median SOG curve. Entry SOG is the median of supported values from −25s through −15s, inclusive, and Exit SOG is the median from +15s through +25s, inclusive; each requires at least 8 of its 11 offsets. Gaps are never filled. Minimum SOG is the minimum from −10s through +10s, inclusive, and requires support at all 21 offsets. A supported offset has at least three maneuver contributors. Entry, Minimum and Exit P/C readings are descriptive: absolute differences of at most 0.2 kt are Similar; otherwise they identify the higher value. A reading is unavailable without both values and makes no overall ranking.
- Profile recovery is separate from the event-table Recovery metric and uses the supported raw median curve. It requires available Entry SOG and an evidenced SOG dip below 95% of Entry. The first supported integer-grid second at or after `t=0` returning to or above that threshold is recovery only when support is continuous from the relevant dip through that crossing; a pre-center dip must have support spanning `t=0`. An already-above-threshold `t=0` without an evidenced preceding dip is not recovery. A continuously supported dip through +25s with no crossing is **not reached by +25s**; absent Entry, dip or needed support is unavailable. P/C recovery times are compared descriptively only when both are established. The visible P/C curves share an adaptive SOG axis: take their supported values, add at least 0.2 kt margin, round outward to whole knots, keep the lower bound nonnegative and the span at least 3 kt. Empty curves use 0–3 kt.

### Artifact and API validity

- The backend persists one private `.maneuvers.json` artifact per Activity with Activity ID, canonical-track SHA-256, shared integer algorithm version **7**, availability status and detected events. An available artifact has exactly one `detector_mode` (`HIGH` or `LOW`) for all its events and an ordered canonical `signal_basis`. LOW uses `cog`, `sog`; HIGH uses required `hdg` plus `cog`, `heel` and/or `sog` actually evaluated in candidate assessments, including rejecting evidence. Unused channels, TRIM and quaternion are not claimed. Available results may contain zero events and have no unavailable reason. Unavailable artifacts have null mode, empty signal basis, a specific reason and no events.
- Artifacts are generated lazily on authorized read and regenerated if missing, malformed, from another version or tied to a different canonical-track hash. Strict validation checks status, mode, signals, reason, angle/rate semantics, finite measures and ordered UTC event boundaries. Version-6 artifacts are stale and recomputed through existing read/generation paths, never relabelled or bulk-recalculated. A current unavailable artifact is reusable. Independent HIGH/LOW research calls do not overwrite the normal selected artifact.
- Original and temporary Session access use capability-scoped, read-only maneuver endpoints for visible member Activities. A caller cannot obtain another Activity's analytics by knowing its ID. [Access and Consent](access-and-consent.md) owns token validity, lifetime and ACTIVE-only authorization; [Session Viewer](session-viewer.md) owns event filtering, selection and presentation.

### Analytical boundary

- The current contract does not include automatic tack/gybe classification, start or leg analysis, tactical gained/lost, VMG, wind reconstruction, performance ranking, scoring, coaching or AI advice. Completed [#112](https://github.com/maxsail-project/tackbar/issues/112) explicitly supersedes the previous HDG-only/no-COG-fallback contract with HIGH-first/LOW selection in version 7, including GPX movement evidence, without changing GPX ingestion. Phases acceptance does not establish recall or equivalence to HIGH; known gradual-turn misses near ESP29375 P02 12:19:30 and 12:33:42 UTC remain manual review cases. Completed [#113](https://github.com/maxsail-project/tackbar/issues/113) adds the Viewer evidence explanation described in [Session Viewer](session-viewer.md), without extending analytical scope.
