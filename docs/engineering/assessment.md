**Highest evidenced cumulative level: L2 — Repeatable.** AI-assisted changes are visible in the sampled Issue/commit record, while the inspected test suites, documented conventions, repeated Issue workflow, and two SHA-linked successful CI runs support L1 and L2. L3 is not consolidated: the complete shared method is not repository-accessible, independent review is mainly evidenced by reports, and contributor adoption without private context has not been tested. This is a qualitative judgment over a small sample, not a certification or claim that all engineering behavior has been audited.

**Higher-level practices, separately:** #211 reports coordinated agents and evidence-controlled checkpoints, and #214 shows a feedback-driven rule change. Reusable skills, demonstrated portability, and measured improvement outcomes remain pending or unverified. These practices do not bypass the missing L3 criteria. The [engineering roadmap](roadmap.md) proposes bounded follow-up work; it grants no implementation authority.
