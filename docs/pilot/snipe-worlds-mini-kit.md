# TackBar — Snipe Worlds Mini Kit

## Goal

Use the Snipe World Championship as a natural environment to introduce and test TackBar with real sailors.

The objective is not mass marketing.

The objective is:

- show TackBar in a real sailing context;
- invite a small number of interested sailors into the pilot;
- run real debriefs;
- learn from how sailors actually use the product;
- create useful conversations around post-sailing analysis.

---

# 1. Core message

## TackBar

**Sail. Debrief. Learn.**

TackBar helps dinghy sailors turn sailing data into a shared debrief.

The idea is simple:

**Sail → review what happened → discuss it together → learn**

---

# 2. 20-second pitch

## English

> TackBar is a simple tool for debriefing sailing sessions together. We take the data from Vakaros, create a shared session, and you can compare tracks, replay what happened and discuss it with your crew or other sailors. The idea is simple: sail, debrief, learn.

## Short version

> TackBar turns your Vakaros sailing data into a shared debrief session. Sail, compare what happened, talk about it, learn.

---

# 3. Demo strategy

Do not start by showing the website.

Start by showing one **real TackBar session** on a phone or tablet.

The ideal demo session should include:

- two boats;
- one clear tactical or performance difference;
- one moment where the boats are close;
- one decision that changes the outcome;
- replay;
- useful comparison metrics;
- a story that can be explained in under two minutes.

Example demo conversation:

> Look here. We were almost together at this point.  
> Then one boat went left.  
> Let’s move the replay forward and see what happened.

The goal is for the sailor to understand TackBar through the sailing story, not through a product explanation.

---

# 4. Pilot flow

Current pilot flow:

**Sail with Vakaros**

↓

**Open the activity in Vakaros Connect**

↓

**Share/email the activity with TackBar**

↓

**TackBar creates a shared session**

↓

**Receive the session link**

↓

**Open it on phone, tablet or laptop**

↓

**Debrief together**

The objective is to make the transition from sailing to debrief as simple as possible.

# 9. Feedback questions

Avoid long surveys during the event.

After a sailor uses TackBar, ask a few simple questions.

## Question 1

> Did the debrief help you understand something you hadn’t seen on the water?

## Question 2

> What did you naturally want to do next in TackBar?

## Question 3

> What felt confusing or unnecessary?

## Key question

> Would you use this again after tomorrow’s racing?

This last question is especially valuable as an early product signal.

---

# 10. Pilot success criteria

Do not measure success mainly by:

- QR scans;
- website visits;
- number of people who see the shirts.

Prefer real product signals.

A very good result would be:

- **5–10 sailors genuinely interested**
- **3–5 real pilot users**
- **several real debrief sessions**
- **useful conversations and observations**
- **2–3 sailors asking to use TackBar again**

The key signal is repeated use.

---

# 12. What not to do

Avoid:

- long product presentations;
- feature lists;
- technical architecture explanations;
- formal sales pitches;
- complex onboarding;
- multiple QR codes;
- large surveys;
- trying to recruit everyone.

TackBar is still a pilot.

Prefer:

**conversation → real example → interest → pilot → real use**

---

# 13. Snipe Worlds opportunity

The Snipe World Championship is a particularly good environment for the pilot because:

- many sailors will be together in one place;
- there will be daily sailing;
- Vakaros devices will be in use;
- sailors naturally discuss races afterwards;
- crews and friends compare decisions and performance;
- repeated sailing days allow repeated product use.

This matches the TackBar philosophy closely:

**Sail. Debrief. Learn.**
